References

Please have your references ready ahead of time. I will not hold a slot while you search for a reference.

Rendered art is not recommended for a color reference. A clear and visible color palette is very useful. It is up to the commissioner to provide at least a moderate quality reference. Highly pixelated images will not be accepted.

If commissions are taken through social media, individual images are welcome, though a gallery link is more helpful and organized. Google Drive and Dropbox are good services with folder/gallery functions.

If you do not have any references, Picrew offers various avatar makers to choose from.
